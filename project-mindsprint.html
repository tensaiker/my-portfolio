PROJECT OVERVIEW

About MindSprint

MindSprint makes everyday arithmetic more engaging through short practice sessions, selectable difficulty levels, timed questions, and game-inspired challenges. Users can work with addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and integers, then review their results and track activity over time.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Key Features

Custom Practice

Choose a math operation, difficulty, question count, and time limit for focused sessions.

Four Game Modes

Take on Rapid 10, Time Attack, Survival, and Perfect 10—each with its own rules and goals.

Progress & Results

Review accuracy, scores, streaks, and practice performance with a dedicated progress area.

DEVELOPMENT REFLECTION

Design & Development Focus

The project brings together math question flows, configurable sessions, time-based challenges, clear result screens, and a consistent cozy interface. The goal was to make repeat practice feel approachable while still giving learners ways to challenge themselves.
