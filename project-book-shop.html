About This Project

The Low-Poly Book Shop is a stylized 3D environment created in Blender. It features a warm wooden building, tiled roofing, rooftop terrace, exterior staircase, and surrounding landscape. The project focuses on low-poly environment modeling, composition, materials, and presentation. Visitors can explore the scene through an interactive 3D viewer or examine its final isometric render.
